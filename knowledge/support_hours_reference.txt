PATIO KITS DIRECT HOURS AND SUPPORT AVAILABILITY

This file teaches Junior Andrew how to explain Patio Kits Direct business hours and build support hours.

Use Pacific Time as the default because Patio Kits Direct is based in California.

GENERAL OFFICE / SALES / CUSTOMER SERVICE HOURS

General Patio Kits Direct office, sales, and customer service hours:
Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm Pacific Time.

Closed Saturday and Sunday for normal general office, sales, and customer service unless a representative has arranged otherwise.

BUILD SUPPORT HOURS

Build support has different hours than the general office.

Build support hours:
Tuesday through Friday, 8am to 5pm Pacific Time.
Saturday, 10am to 2pm Pacific Time.

Build support is normally closed Sunday and Monday.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Do not say "Patio Kits Direct is closed" in a way that hides the build support schedule.

Better:
"General office hours are Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm Pacific Time. Build support runs Tuesday through Friday, 8am to 5pm, and Saturday from 10am to 2pm Pacific Time."

If the customer asks about sales, quotes, ordering, payment, delivery, pickup, warranty, or general customer service:
Use general office hours.

If the customer asks about installation, missing build details, construction questions, how to assemble something, or help during the build:
Use build support hours.

GOOD ANSWERS

Customer asks:
"What are your hours?"

Good answer:
"General Patio Kits Direct office hours are Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm Pacific Time. Build support is Tuesday through Friday, 8am to 5pm, and Saturday from 10am to 2pm Pacific Time."

Customer asks:
"Is build support open Saturday?"

Good answer:
"Yes, build support is normally available Saturday from 10am to 2pm Pacific Time."

Customer asks:
"Can I call about my quote Saturday?"

Good answer:
"General sales and quote support is normally Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm Pacific Time. Build support has Saturday hours, but quote and order questions are usually best handled during general office hours."

Customer asks:
"I am building right now. Can I get help?"

Good answer:
"Build support is normally Tuesday through Friday, 8am to 5pm Pacific Time, and Saturday from 10am to 2pm. If you are outside those hours, I can give general guidance, but build support should confirm anything project-specific when they reopen."

AFTER HOURS WORDING

If the customer asks a project-specific question outside the relevant support hours, give general guidance only and tell them to confirm with the right team when they reopen.

For sales, quote, order, delivery, pickup, warranty, or payment questions:
"Since general office support is closed right now, I can give the general idea, but a representative should confirm when Patio Kits Direct reopens."

For build or installation questions:
"Since build support is closed right now, I can give general guidance, but build support should confirm anything project-specific when they reopen."

Do not pretend a live representative is available if that team is closed.
