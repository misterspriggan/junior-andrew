COMPANY FACTS V2

Company Name

Patio Kits Direct

Business Model

Patio Kits Direct is a DIY patio cover supplier.

DIY means Do It Yourself.

The company supplies patio cover materials and kits directly to customers.

Patio Kits Direct does not provide installation crews.

Patio Kits Direct does not employ installers.

Patio Kits Direct does not manage installations.

Customers may install the project themselves.

Customers may hire a contractor of their choice.

Installer Network

Patio Kits Direct sells materials to contractors and installers throughout the United States.

Some customers may be able to find local contractors or installers who have purchased materials from Patio Kits Direct.

However:

Patio Kits Direct does not personally know every installer.

Patio Kits Direct does not personally vet every installer.

Patio Kits Direct does not supervise installers.

Patio Kits Direct does not manage third-party installations.

Patio Kits Direct does not guarantee the quality of any third-party installer.

Customers should use their own judgment when hiring contractors.

Installation Rules

Never claim Patio Kits Direct has installation crews.

Never claim Patio Kits Direct sends installers.

Never claim Patio Kits Direct installs locally.

Never claim Patio Kits Direct manages construction projects.

Never claim Patio Kits Direct guarantees third-party contractors.

Never claim Patio Kits Direct has authorized installers.

If a customer asks whether Patio Kits Direct installs, explain that Patio Kits Direct supplies the materials and the customer can install the kit themselves or hire a contractor of their choice.

If a customer asks for installer recommendations, explain that Patio Kits Direct may sell materials to contractors, but does not personally vet or guarantee third-party installers.

Service Area

Patio Kits Direct sells materials in approved service states.

Refer to the service area file for current coverage.

Materials availability is not the same as installation availability.

Do not answer service area questions based on installer availability.

Do not answer service area questions based on contractor availability.

Answer service area questions based on whether the customer's city or state is in the supported material service area.
