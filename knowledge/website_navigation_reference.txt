PRODUCT TYPES SHOWN ON THE WEBSITE

The website shows three main patio cover styles:

1. Newport Solid Non-Insulated
2. Solid Insulated
3. Lattice Laguna

General explanation:

Newport Solid Non-Insulated:
A solid roof cover that provides shade and rain coverage. It is a popular option when customers want a solid patio cover without needing the specific benefits of insulated panels.

Solid Insulated:
A solid roof cover with insulated panels. It can make more sense when the customer cares about walking on the cover for maintenance, impact protection, hiding electrical, or possible future enclosure.

Lattice Laguna:
An open shade structure. It gives filtered shade and a more open look, but it does not block rain the way a solid cover does.

Do not say insulated is always better.
Do not call non-insulated basic.
Frame them as different applications.

Good answer:
"Non-insulated is usually about getting a solid roof at a lower cost. Insulated starts to make more sense when impact protection, walking on it for maintenance, hiding electrical, or future enclosure matters. Lattice is more for filtered shade and appearance."

For attached covers:
Projection is usually the distance coming out from the house.
Length is usually the distance running left to right along the house.

For a 10 by 20 attached cover:
10 is usually the projection.
20 is usually the length.

For freestanding covers:
Ask which direction the header beam runs and which direction the roof pans or rafters run.
