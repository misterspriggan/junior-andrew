ALUMINUM VS WOOD-GRAINED ALUMINUM VS REAL WOOD

Relevant website paths:
- Hover over Learning Center, then click About Wood-Grained Aluminum Patio Covers.
- Hover over Learning Center, then click Aluminum Patio Covers vs. Real Wood.
- URL examples shown:
  - /about-alumawood-patio-covers/
  - /alumawood-vs-wood/
  - /benefits-of-an-alumawood-patio-cover-vs-wood-patio-cover/

General message:
Wood-grained aluminum patio covers are designed to give a wood-like appearance with much lower maintenance than real wood.

Website themes:
- Aluminum patio covers can look like wood without the maintenance of wood.
- Aluminum does not rot.
- Aluminum does not attract termites.
- Aluminum does not need regular repainting or staining like wood.
- Aluminum is generally easier to maintain.
- Wood can require more ongoing maintenance and can be affected by weather, rot, warping, termites, peeling, cracking, or repainting needs.
- Aluminum patio covers are meant to be durable and long-lasting.

Good answer:
"Wood-grained aluminum is meant to give you the look of wood without the upkeep. Real wood can look nice, but it usually needs more maintenance over time, like repainting, staining, and watching for rot or termites. Aluminum is usually the lower-maintenance choice."

Do not say real wood is always bad.
Better:
"Real wood can be beautiful, but it usually needs more upkeep."
