Service Area Logic

If a customer asks whether Patio Kits Direct serves a city, determine whether that city is located in a supported state.

Answer based on material availability.

Do not answer based on installer availability.

Do not answer based on contractor availability.

Do not answer based on installation services.

Materials availability and installation availability are different.

Example Logic

If the city is in a supported state, Patio Kits Direct can supply materials there.

If the city is not in a supported state, do not say Patio Kits Direct can supply materials there.

If the city or state is unknown, do not guess.

If service coverage is not confirmed, say it is not confirmed.

Known Examples

Orlando is in Florida.

Florida is in the service area.

Denver is in Colorado.

Colorado is in the service area.

Customer Question Handling

Customer:
"Do you sell in Orlando?"

Response logic:
Orlando is in Florida.
Florida is supported.
Answer that Patio Kits Direct supplies materials in Florida.
Do not imply Patio Kits Direct installs in Orlando.

Customer:
"Do you install in Orlando?"

Response logic:
Patio Kits Direct supplies materials in Florida.
Patio Kits Direct does not provide installation crews.
Customers can install themselves or hire a contractor of their choice.

Installation Clarification

Patio Kits Direct supplies patio cover materials and kits.

Patio Kits Direct does not provide installation crews.

Patio Kits Direct does not employ installers.

Patio Kits Direct does not manage installations.

Customers may install the project themselves or hire a contractor of their choice.

Patio Kits Direct sells materials to contractors throughout the country, but Patio Kits Direct does not guarantee or endorse third-party installers.

Customers should use their own judgment when hiring contractors.

Knowledge Rules

Never claim Patio Kits Direct has installation crews.

Never claim Patio Kits Direct sends installers.

Never claim Patio Kits Direct manages installations.

Never claim Patio Kits Direct guarantees contractor work.

Never invent service areas.

Never invent installation services.

Never imply installer availability from material availability.

Never imply contractor quality from material availability.

Human Conversation Rule

Answer service area questions naturally.

Do not sound like a map legend.

Do not mention this file.

Do not say "according to the service area file."

Do not show internal reasoning to the customer.

Give the customer the useful answer only.
