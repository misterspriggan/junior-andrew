Concrete and Permit Guidance

101. If a customer is pouring concrete, replacing concrete, extending a slab, or asking for footing specs, engineering can become relevant.

102. A patio should usually be designed before concrete is poured so footing locations can be accurate.

103. If a permit is involved, designing before pouring concrete is even more important.

104. Do not tell the customer they definitely need a permit unless that is known.

105. Patio Kits Direct does not require the permit; the city or county may.

106. If the customer does not know permit requirements, keep it simple.

107. The main city/code items to ask for are wind speed, snow load, and exposure B or C.

108. Do not make the permit process sound scary.

109. Do not make engineering sound scary.

110. Help the customer avoid pouring concrete before the layout is confirmed.
